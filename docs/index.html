bullet.ly

A bullet-journal style weekly planner for Firefox

bullet.ly is a personal Firefox extension that turns your week into a bullet-journal style spread on grid paper. It shows your Google Calendar events, lets you keep a to-do list for each day using Google Tasks, and lets you decorate the page with stickers, photos, notes, drawings and a habit tracker.

What it does with your Google account

  • Google Calendar (read-only): displays your events and calendars in the weekly view. It cannot change or delete events.
  • Google Tasks (read and write): shows your to-dos on the day they're due, and lets you check them off, add them and rename them.

Your data goes straight from Google to your own browser. There is no bullet.ly server, and nothing is collected, sold or shared. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Features

  • Weekly timetable styled like a bullet journal, with a to-do row above each day
  • Monthly view with color-filled days, plus free-style pages you design yourself with boxes, washi tape, banners, checklists and trackers
  • Habit tracker and weekly goals
  • Stickers, your own photos, rich text notes, and freehand pen and highlighter drawing
  • Custom fonts
