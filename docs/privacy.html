Privacy Policy

bullet.ly · last updated 7 October 2026

bullet.ly ("the app") is a Firefox extension that displays your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks in a bullet-journal style weekly planner. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and approve access, the app can read:

  • Google Calendar (read-only): the list of your calendars (names and colors) and the events in the week you're viewing (title, time, and link).
  • Google Tasks (read and write): your task lists and tasks (title, due date, status). The app creates, renames, completes and deletes tasks only when you do so in the app.
  • The email address of your primary calendar, used only as a sign-in hint (see below).

How the information is used

It is used only to display your calendar and to-do list to you inside the app, and to carry out the task changes you make. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Where it goes

Data travels directly between Google and your own browser. The app has no server and the developer never receives, stores or has access to your data. There are no analytics or trackers. Your information is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone.

What is stored, and where

Everything below stays in your own Firefox profile (browser storage) on your computer:

  • Your Google sign-in access token (valid for about one hour) and your own OAuth Client ID.
  • The email hint mentioned above, and your settings: fonts, hidden calendars, habits, goals, stickers, photos, notes and drawings.

Calendar events and tasks are held in memory while the page is open and are not saved to disk by the app.

Your choices

  • Disconnect inside the app revokes the access token with Google and clears the sign-in data.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing the extension from Firefox deletes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

bullet.ly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
